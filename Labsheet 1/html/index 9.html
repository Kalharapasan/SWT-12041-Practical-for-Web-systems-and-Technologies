Superscriptand Subscript ❼ A subscript (like H2O) or superscript (like x2) is a character (such
as a number or letter) that is set slightly below or above the normal line of type, respectively. It is
usually smaller than the rest of the text. Subscripts appear at or below the baseline, while
superscripts are above. Subscripts and superscripts are perhaps most often used in formulas,
mathematical expressions, and specifications of chemical compounds and isotopes, but have
many other uses as well.
